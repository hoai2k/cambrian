The Aphaneramma reference depicts **Metoposaurus**, and the Odontochelys reference depicts **Thaichelys**. Those are deliberately used for body, limb, skin and color direction; they do not override the target species' defining anatomy. Aphaneramma keeps its extreme narrow rostrum. Odontochelys keeps its teeth, long tail and belly-only shell with a bare ribbed back. Cymbospondylus follows a reconstruction of the same species.

The generated candidates change pose, rendering, environment and anatomy as detailed in the prompts. Preserve these credits and the source license information with any distribution of the reference files or adaptations; no endorsement by the artists is implied.
