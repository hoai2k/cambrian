## What the shore animals cost, and what they taught

Landed in review state on `main`, not in `shipped.json`, so nothing broken is in the game. Two real
defects are recorded rather than hidden (tasks #27, #28):

- **The limb skinning tears**, and the paired audits *structurally cannot see it* — they check where
  each vertex is, and travel from rest stays bounded the whole time while a foot is pulled inside
  out. `tools/triassic/skin-tears.mjs` measures the real thing, each triangle edge against its rest
  length over every clip. Sweep: Nothosaurus 2.98x (clean, the reference), Tanystropheus 6.1x,
  Placodus 12.4x, Macrocnemus 23.3x, Coelophysis 25.3x — whose hind feet trail off in ribbons at
  gameplay scale. It tracks how hard a limb is swung, so it is weights to fix, not inherent.
- **Macrocnemus' mandible is a sliver**, because the jaw hinge was taken from the skull bone. The fix
  was attempted and deliberately not shipped, because it exposes a worse one: the throat does not
  follow the jaw, which is the two-separate-tubes failure the oral-lining rule exists to prevent.

Three more things worth not relearning: `shorekit.Albedo` had gone missing and needed an sRGB decode
to reconstruct; **Blender exits 0 when a builder raises**, so a failed build reports success and
leaves stale artefacts looking fresh (`shorekit.py` now installs an excepthook); and the strike named
the wrong side, invisible to every check because the hit lands either way.

## The rate-limit kill, 23:10 UTC, and what it exposed

All five running agents died at once on a session-wide 429. Nothing they did caused it; five
concurrent Opus agents did. Two lessons, both cheap and both learned the expensive way:

- **Agents do not commit often enough.** Not one of the three long-running agents had committed
  *anything* — every branch was 0 ahead of `main` and all the work was untracked files in an
  ephemeral container, an hour or more of Blender output each. It was rescued by committing from the
  parent session and pushing: `c3c8e93`, `93b7303`, `43f7e06` on the three `worktree-agent-*`
  branches, all on `origin`. Had the container been reclaimed first it would simply have been gone.
  A build that takes an hour should commit as it goes, not at the end.
- **Do not run five at once.** Three concurrent is the working number; the two that had barely
  started (Henodus/Keichousaurus, Aphaneramma/Mystriosuchus) were deliberately *not* resumed and
  wait for a slot.

Resuming beats respawning: `SendMessage` to a dead agent's id brings it back with its transcript, so
it continues rather than rediscovering. All three were resumed with a note saying where their work
had been committed to, and told to build on the rescue commit.

## Standing rules established this session

All are in `CLAUDE.md` unless noted.

- **Tripo is fed one view of one animal, never a contact sheet.** A six-panel sheet fed whole came
  back as six turtles in one GLB. The four-view turnaround is a human review artefact.
- **A playable Triassic animal swims.** Generations posed for land still get the swim set as their
  locomotion; a walk is an extra. Shore animals are the exception, being unplayable.
- **A creature merges to `main` when it is finished, not when its batch is.**
- **A mouth must read as a mouth, not a hole**: one closed skinned lining wound inwards, skin
  double-sided behind it; the jaw seam fitted to the model's own lip contour; three anchors with
  the attack anchor on the bone that delivers the blow. Proving it needs a cull-shim comparison —
  comparing against the plain background measures the backdrop and always passes.
- **A limbed swimmer's dash has to paddle**, swept angle per limb root recorded as a number.
- **A subject whose era is undecided stays off the roster**, because roster membership is what puts
  an animal in the sea.
- **What may be authored on a Tripo body is decided by how simple the shape is**, and must wear the
  creature's own texture.
- Which pipeline steps are judgement and which are mechanical: `docs/triassic/04-tripo-pipeline.md`.

## Environment notes worth not rediscovering

- Blender 5.2.1 at `/opt/blender/blender`. **EEVEE and Workbench need EGL, which this container does
  not have**, so `tools/triassic/tripo/review.py` fails at its render step. Its `audit` and
  `export_preview` are fine: import the module and call them directly rather than editing the tool
  (there is a working example of this in the Archelon intake).
- Raw Tripo bodies are unstitched patch soup. Weld at 0.0005 before any connected-component or
  Laplacian work, or you measure the triangulation instead of the animal.
- The Tripo v3 API has **no task-listing endpoint**; `/v3/tasks/{uuid}` is all there is, so a task
  id that was not written down is gone. A generation costs 30 credits.
- The API key used this session is in the session transcript and **should be rotated**.
