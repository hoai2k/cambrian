# Triassic raster and mark assets

`biomes/` contains text-free 16:9 art for the nine Triassic ocean biomes. The
PNG files are retained as lossless source masters; the matching `.webp` files are
the runtime delivery assets. Their prompts and art direction are recorded in
`tools/triassic/environment-image-prompts.json`.

`brand/title.webp` and `brand/title-mobile.webp` are engraved parchment title
plates, derived directly from Devonian's title style. `brand/logo-engraved.webp`
is the transparent wordmark; the full-body four-flipper plesiosaur `emblem.webp`
supplies every favicon and touch icon. SVG compatibility files embed this art.
Earlier blue text-free `keyart*` files are supplementary scenery, not title
masters. Sources, references, prompts and reproduction details are documented in
`docs/triassic-brand-assets.md`.
