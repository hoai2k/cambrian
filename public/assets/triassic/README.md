# Triassic raster and mark assets

`biomes/` contains text-free 16:9 art for the nine Triassic ocean biomes. The
PNG files are retained as lossless source masters; the matching `.webp` files are
the runtime delivery assets. Their prompts and art direction are recorded in
`tools/triassic/environment-image-prompts.json`.

`brand/keyart.webp` and `brand/keyart-mobile.webp` are intentionally text-free
marine title backdrops. `brand/title.webp` and `brand/title-mobile.webp` are the
ready-composed equivalents; `brand/title.svg` and `brand/title-mobile.svg` remain
available for responsive composition. The wordmark follows the Cambrian and Devonian
engraved black serif system, with ivory and gold edging, but its framing remains
underwater: shells, reef texture, and surface light rather than a land-dinosaur
poster.
