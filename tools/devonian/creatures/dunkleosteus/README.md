# Dunkleosteus terrelli

Original Devonian specimen artwork authored separately for this subject. Source mesh is generated with Blender 5.2, with a rigid armoured forebody and a separately weighted muscular rear body. No Cambrian geometry or downloaded model is reused.

## Anatomical direction

Late Devonian Cleveland Shale, Ohio. This is a representative approximately 3.35 m adult interpretation, not a genus maximum. The short, deep-bodied outline, prominent cranial/thoracic armour, broad anterior pectoral fins and gnathal cutting structures follow the reconstruction framework discussed by [Engelman 2024](https://www.palaeo-electronica.org/content/2024/5307-dunkleosteus-reconstruction). The representative scale is consistent with the revised length framework in [Engelman 2023](https://doi.org/10.3390/d15030318).

Known armour and jaw anatomy receive the greatest emphasis. The posterior body and heterocercal caudal outline are comparative reconstructions; fin membranes, living armour boundaries, oral soft covering, skin pores and pigmentation remain interpretations. The model is not a digitized specimen or a research-grade plate-suture diagram. In particular, the thin submarginal cheek element is indicated, but its living tissue coverage is uncertain. Gnathal structures are paired cutting plates rather than rows of shark teeth.

## Construction and materials

The main body is a smooth interpolated elliptical loft with posterior skin weights. Head and mandible have separate shaped surfaces. Cranial and thoracic plates are tessellated and projected onto the actual underlying surface, avoiding the protruding flat panels identified in the first review. Paired pectoral fins have flexible distal controls, raised radial supports and thickened membranes; pelvic, dorsal, anal and caudal fins have their own controls. Eyes are small, dark and reflective.

`skin-albedo.png` was produced with the built-in imagegen tool. Its pigment field is sampled into the mesh vertex colours, preserving runtime recolouring and keeping the entire appearance independent of external textures. `dermal-normal.png` is a deterministic, fine tangent normal generated by the builder and embedded in GLB materials. Neither texture is fossil evidence.

## Reproduction

Run from the repository root:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --threads 2 --python tools/devonian/creatures/dunkleosteus/build.py
python3 tools/devonian/creatures/dunkleosteus/finalize.py
```

The finalizer uses NumPy. Blender may require execution outside the macOS sandbox because Metal initialization crashes in this environment. It uses Cycles CPU rendering with two threads. Exporting generates identity scale tracks automatically; the finalizer verifies that they are constant identity before removing them, and removes constant root tracks. It does not alter animated anatomical transforms.

Editable source and full-size reviews: `cambrian/local/devonian-authoring/dunkleosteus/dunkleosteus.blend`, `reference-board.png`, `appearance-concept.png`, `anatomy-detail.png`, `action-review.png`, and the nine individual action renders. The reference board explicitly labels its pictures as views of our reconstruction, not fossil photographs.

## Animation direction

All 18 full-model actions have independent jaw/fin/tail articulation at 30 fps. The root is stationary; body movement is local. Armour remains bound rigidly to head/body and never follows posterior tail bones. The four seamless loops are Idle, Swim, Guard and Eat. Death holds its terminal side-roll pose. Growth is a relaxed display without body scaling or moulting. Action labels provide compatibility and do not define Devonian game rules.

| Clip | Seconds | Authored gesture |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Idle | 2.4 | Restrained caudal correction, breathing jaw and pectoral balance |
| Swim | 2.4 | Two stronger caudal cycles with delayed posterior wave and paired fin trim |
| TurnLeft / TurnRight | 1.6 | Bank and steer with asymmetric pectorals and counter-curved tail |
| Dive / Rise | 1.4 | Body pitch, head compensation and independent fin-plane changes |
| Attack | 1.0 | Anticipatory recoil, cranial elevation, jaw opening and closing lunge |
| Bite | 0.5 | Shorter, smaller cutting-jaw snap |
| Heavy | 1.1 | Larger jaw excursion with pectoral bracing and heavy closing recoil |
| Hit | 0.6 | Single destabilized recoil sequence |
| Death | 1.6 | Progressive lateral roll, settling fins, slack jaw and held pose |
| Guard | 1.0 | Broad pectoral brace with breathing and tail balance |
| Parry | 0.367 | Short bank, cranial brace and opposite caudal response |
| Dodge | 0.4 | Local lateral thrust, bank and tail counter-bend |
| Eat | 1.2 | Seamless jaw-processing cycle with subdued fin corrections |
| Stagger | 1.2 | Longer repeated recoil oscillation and asymmetric balance |
| Ability | 1.8 | Deliberate large gape followed by a separate smaller closing gesture |
| Growth | 1.5 | Relaxed head lift, fin spread and mild jaw movement |

Parry is rounded to eleven 30 fps frame intervals. Non-loop actions recover their initial pose except Death. No grasping anatomy is invented, so no Grab action is included.

## Export and review results

- Full: 26,448 triangles, 14,532 exported vertices, 18 clips, 1,903,740 bytes.
- LOD: 7,438 triangles, 4,766 vertices, three retained clips (Idle, Swim, Death), 1,060,276 bytes. Triangle count is 28.12% of full.
- Both retain 16 skeleton joints and the same three non-deforming sockets: `anchor_mouth`, `anchor_mouth_inside`, `anchor_attack_primary`. They are attached to the jaw; nested `cambrianAnchor` v1 metadata is exported as an object. These fixed jaw contacts deliberately have no fabricated CCD chain.
- glTF +Z forward, +Y up. Measured bind length: 3.340000 m. Model width includes spread pectoral fins.
- `validation.json` records decoded geometry, weight normalization, finite transforms, stable root, no scale channels, nonzero and distinct motion, seamless loop endpoints, socket positions against the Blender world manifest, and actual LOD reduction.
- Reviewed the final nine-action sheet in side, frontal and three-quarter views. Corrected floating armour, exaggerated pore normals, overly bright eye rims and an exposed-looking oral core. Final poses show intact armour fit and legible mouth articulation. Remaining limitations are the explicitly inferred anatomical and material details above.

The four public PNGs are rendered from the final full source: studio and selection 1600×1200 RGBA, card 800×600 RGBA, thumbnail 256×192 RGBA. No generated concept is used as a substitute for the final rendered specimen.
