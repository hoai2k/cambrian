# Candidate01 actual visual review — rejected for revision

Inspected all eight actual 1000×800 PNGs individually: rest-side, rest-oblique, rest-front, max-oblique, max-front, mid-bite-side, eat-oblique and recovery-oblique. Compared against the supplied user JPG and previously inspected actual V2 side/gape/eye views. Exact PNG and evidence hashes are recorded in `candidate01-reviewed-evidence.json`. Candidate Blend SHA256 is `d55f9141e06ebb2de85e3f1a7ff952544c30eb2b7d571bf10d24e2ef14237eed`.

The candidate is not acceptable. All views show crumpling around the anterior brow and snout, including sharp black triangular artifacts. The broad cheek is fuller but its contour still does not read cleanly; the old mapped suture partly competes with the sculpt. Candidate01 queried vertex normals while mutating the same mesh and pushed a narrow mask on limited support topology; this is a suspected cause of the forehead defects, not a proven diagnosis.

The anterior lower points and pointed upper gnathals are more distinctive at maximal gape and Eat, improving the intended cutting silhouette. However, both the mid-bite side and eating views reveal very tall slab-like continuous lower walls. Rest front/oblique and recovery show dark lower root surfaces extending beneath/outside the mandibular envelope. The posterior upper blade looks like a deep rectangular hanging plate in open oblique views. Broad flat-shaded panels and repeated row segmentation exaggerate the artificial appearance. At rest the lower cusps disappear under the cheek; the images cannot establish clearance from palate/head. This does not pass mouth contact review.

The preserved mucosal floor, cheek lining and recessed throat remain visible and continuous in maximal frontal gape. The body and fins retain their accepted appearance, and the evidence report confirms unchanged hashes for them, the lower jaw shell, mouth lining, eye globes, rig, anchors, action curves and packed image bytes. This preservation is useful but does not approve the changed face or gnathals.

Candidate02 is a bounded revision from the same frozen original V2 input. It adds one support subdivision to the head, snapshots input coordinates/normals before displacement, broadens brow relief, softens the narrow seam and reduces roof lift. It ray-fits gnathal roots into the actual preserved lower-jaw shell and the new upper head support, narrows posterior lower bases, reduces the lower anterior tip slightly, and uses smooth root shading with a thin chisel edge. Cross-section landmarks use linear interpolation to avoid repeated near-flat slope steps. The terminal upper leading section extrapolates at most 20 mm from its sampled supporting root. Root fitting is a design improvement, not a substitute for fresh actual pose inspection.

Candidate01 source and local outputs are retained. Candidate02 requires a fresh root-managed CPU2 build and all eight image reviews before any further verdict. Eye volume, complete motion contacts, anchors, full/LOD and portrait/intake checks remain pending; neither candidate has final QA.
