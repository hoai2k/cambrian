Execution passed, backup SHA unchanged. **Reject this as the production form.**
The old model retains more organic appeal in several views; it remains the fallback.

Useful changes: the fuller, rising tail axis, lower/longer dorsal and a separately readable
armoured thorax move toward the request. Keep those broad intentions. However, angularity
has been applied too uniformly and aggressively; the result is a block-shaped toy fish.

- The front is almost rectangular, with a straight-sided tall helmet, flat forehead, blunt cut
  muzzle and a vertical step into the thorax. The user reference has a substantial rounded snout,
  curved cheek and organically angled armour. Sculpt region-specific convexity and transitions;
  do not apply a single faceted cross-section around every ring from snout to tail.
- The shield is a block sleeve around the body with a deep notched rear interface. The reference
  has a flowing, rounded dorsal contour and differentiated broad plate curvature. Reduce the
  step and notch, blend the soft abdominal shoulder, and reserve sharper angles for meaningful
  plate boundaries. A future texture cannot repair the current silhouette.
- The soft posterior looks like a long ridged prism, especially in oblique view. Maintain the
  rising, deeper peduncle but round its muscle mass and vary compression continuously. An
  angular tail is not a polygonal rod.
- The mouth needs a new anatomical construction. Rest view shows a circular black hole at the
  midline, squared upper-lip cuts and grey zigzag fragments on the lower rim. Open view has an
  enormous black cavity, straight vertical side wall and hard rectangular hinge panel. Replace
  artificial clipping/notch artifacts with clean lip/jaw contours, an actual curved palate,
  oral floor and compliant cheek. Do not turn the entire head into a rectangular trap door.
- Pectoral fins are broad sheet paddles with upturned hooks in front view. In the user's image
  they are more restrained and swept. Retain a curved root, taper thickness smoothly, and
  remove end-hook construction artifacts. Small pelvic tips also need a continuous outline.
- The dorsal comparison cameras cut the head and tail off in both images. Correct framing in
  the next fixed view configuration so the complete animal is visible. Do not redo all old
  comparisons merely to reproduce already known evidence.

This is the coarse-form rework gate, not the deferred eye/general audit suite. Address these
obvious sculpt/oral defects before material and animation production. Eye seating will still
need its completed-rework audit later. Preserve clay01 and author a new immutable clay02 group
with complete side/front/dorsal/oblique views plus a focused mouth-open study.
