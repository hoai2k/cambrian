## Authored material-01 direction

The new script opens the frozen clay-04 blend read-only and writes a separate material study.
It adds no independent plates. Ten curved, individually named regional suture paths describe
rostral roofing, the roof-to-orbital/temporal boundary, postorbital/cheek division, posterior
cheek and thoracic dorsal/lateral/lower-shoulder junctions. These interpret anatomical regions
from the reference and existing source discussion; they are not an exact fossil plate trace.
They do not form a repeated grid. Shallow incision, rounded margins and broad local convexity
modify the existing outer skin. Orbital surroundings, the lip and all oral vertices are masked
from displacement. Key displacements follow the same cranial/jaw/body weights.

Muted ochre/bronze armour receives fine dermal granulation and a rougher surface. The flexible
back is cooler slate/olive with a lighter belly, irregular short oblique bars and scattered
flecks. Those marks are colour only: no washboard relief, large overlapping scales or raised
body striping. Fins receive curved fan-oriented pigment, graded roots and restrained microdetail.
A muted iris is painted on the existing recessed globes without adding geometry or a lid ring.
Pigmentation and soft-tissue microdetail are artistic interpretations, not known living colours.

The report must prove that all actual loaded oral key coordinates, weights and faces are
unchanged before/after relief. The procedural study shaders are editable but need a later bake
before glTF export and authored/default game palette checks. No material export or palette
