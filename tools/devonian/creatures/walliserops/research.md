# Walliserops trifurcatus reconstruction research

Primary anatomy: Gishlick & Fortey2023, PNAS120:e2119970120, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2119970120 . PDF and directly inspected figs1–3 retained in local v2/references. Figure1A is topotype UA13447; Fig2A HMNS PI1810 documents flattened keeled tines. We use the long elevated trifurcatus form, not short-hafted hammi, tridens or lindoei. Slight asymmetric central branching follows normal variation. The trident remains rigid on the cephalon; its soft contents are inferred, not preserved. Combat is the authors' interpretation, not established behavior. They report no known enrolled Walliserops specimens; no verified closed enrollment is claimed here.

From the specimen dorsal view, the initial shell represents eleven thoracic segments, long backward genal spines, axial and pleural projections, and five paired plus a terminal pygidial spine. Finer curvature and spine-root transitions remain preview refinement. Surface colour is artistic.

Comparative soft parts: Siveter et al.2021, https://doi.org/10.1002/spp2.1401 (Dalmanites). Biramous appendages, gnathobases and lamellae are reconstructed by relationship, not fossil Walliserops soft tissues. Shared construction utilities from our Eldredgeops original model are explicitly reused for this comparative underside and rig, while special_anatomy.py builds the unique projections and action changes author trident contact/stance gestures.

Comparative eyes: Clarkson et al.2006, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asd.2006.08.002 . Schizochroal optical arrangement supported by phacopid anatomy; the 82 lenses and17 files in this preview are an illustrative array inherited from our comparative utility, NOT a measured Walliserops lens count. Each actual lens solid is independently audited for embedding. No decorative hoop supplies counted head volume.

Original Morzadec2001 taxonomic monograph was not directly inspected. 5.5cm shell is representative illustration rather than a species maximum; trident expands total length. Sex cannot be directly established from this reconstruction.
