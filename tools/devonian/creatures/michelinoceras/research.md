# Michelinoceras specimen and uncertainty sheet

## Chosen Devonian taxon
*Michelinoceras currens* (Barrande,1866), specimen Modena19380, Gnoli1982 Plate2Fig1 (section at x3), Lower Devonian SW Sardinia. Four phragmocone fragments, best28mm. This actual primary plate was inspected; commercial polished “Orthoceras” imagery was not used.

Gnoli describes circular cross-section, a smooth exterior, near7° expansion, simple transverse sutures, chambers .8–.9diameter high and declining adorally, septa approximately one-third diameter deep, and a narrow subcentral siphuncle. Reported5–7° variability warrants taxonomic revision. This preview completes a model conch at7° and uses a .5m working living length from the project scale study, **not a measured complete specimen**. Last living chamber length and aperture soft-tissue shape remain unpreserved here. Internal septa are a reference interpretation, not visible surface ribs.

Primary: [Gnoli1982](https://www.paleoitalia.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/05_Gnoli.pdf), pp81–82 and Plate2. LocalPDF/plate/text in v1/references.

## Soft body and motion
[Kröger,Klug&Mapes2005](https://www.app.pan.pl/archive/published/app50/app50-329.pdf) compares Devonian orthocerid internal attachment scars. Weak annular attachment/dorsal furrows suggest restrained soft-part retraction rather than powerful squid-like musculature; those studied taxa do not preserve M.currens full soft anatomy. [Peterman&Ritterbush2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8288114/) evaluates idealized orthocone vertical movement and hydrostatic stability; this motivates restrained whole-shell trim plus funnel-driven escape. Game forward convention is a bind-pose convenience, not evidence of persistent horizontal life position.

Mehl1984, DOI10.1007/BF02986061, reports Michelinoceras sp. Silurian Bolivia radula/arm impressions. Its full paper was unavailable in this session; bibliographic record only verified, and disputed wider claims about ten arms/two long tentacles are **not asserted as observed anatomy**. We use ten similarly proportioned tapering arms as an editable conservative comparative reconstruction, no suckers/hooks or enlarged capture clubs. Funnel, small recessed paired eyes, oral jaws/lining and subtle arm adhesive ridges are comparative soft parts, not established for this Devonian specimen. Pigment and skin texture are artistic. No aptychopsis closure plate invented without species-specific evidence.
