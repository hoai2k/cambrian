# Titanichthys clay03 — structural correction

The source is a fresh candidate built from the successful posterior/fin scaffold in clay02. No saved mesh is imported. The previous candidate and its four actual renders remain preserved. This is an execution plan awaiting visible evidence, not an approved sculpture.

I inspected the user's actual `/Users/hoai/Downloads/Titanichthys.webp` and all four clay02 renders. The reference has a continuous domed head/shoulder silhouette, curved skin-covered armour, clean orbit, long swept fins, and a jaw distinctly smaller than the cheek/throat mass. Its tooth-like cutting edges are not adopted: the fossil-supported Titanichthys jaw remains slender and toothless.

The rejected source projected almost an entire local skin patch onto each tangent plane, then blended the patch abruptly near its polygon edges. This displaced neighbouring patches independently by as much as 0.14 units and generated steps, intersecting folds, and a masonry-like silhouette. Clay03 removes that mechanism entirely. Its cranial and thoracic volume is carried by one smooth longitudinal profile and rounded cross sections. Six curved suture paths make shallow U-shaped incisions, capped at 0.006 units; they cannot create a raised plate block. Sutures avoid the orbital patch. The head/thorax remain broad and deep with a low nuchal crown, cheek buttress and rooted shoulder.

The preoral roof rises through a short rounded profile directly above the lip. This reduces the long flat triangular rostral overhang. The mouth remains a small resting slit, with an anterior downturned lower margin and commissures that sweep back beneath the cheeks. Eye globes retain small radii and recessed placement, with no added rim or iris badge.

The new jaw is a closed U-shaped rail with a diameter of 0.054 units anteriorly and 0.072 units at its joint ends. It follows a transverse hinge near the commissures. It has no cutting blade, tusk, crushing plate, teeth or filter apparatus. This rail is a separate object, partly embedded in the lower oral margin, with its own 24-degree gape shape. In contrast, the outer oral floor behind it is a soft sling: its jaw-following motion eases toward zero ahead of the fixed throat and its middle unfolds slightly downwards. The interior floor follows compatible spatial weights. Thus neither a large ventral armour plate nor the thorax is assigned to the moving jaw. All of these tissue choices remain a reconstruction hypothesis.

Clay02's posterior stations, caudal support, tail outline, paired-fin sweep and resolved endpoints remain. Paired-fin thickness is reduced and falls with a steeper span exponent, preserving buried fleshy roots while making the exposed membrane thinner. The neutral material and lights remain comparable to clay02. The skull's small opening lift is reduced from 3 to 2 degrees; this same transform drives the small eye followers.

Acceptance requires the four actual renders. Look specifically for a flowing roof silhouette, smooth orbital/cheek surfaces, a rounded snout above a quiet toothless margin, a slender moving jaw with a soft floor behind it, continuity at commissures, and thin but believable fins. Reject any new collar/double-lip appearance, film crossing the jaw, sagging or collapsed floor, unnaturally inflated smooth head, or loss of the broad armour character. No textures, skeleton/actions, exports or public intake before explicit root/Astra review of the saved blend and render hashes.
