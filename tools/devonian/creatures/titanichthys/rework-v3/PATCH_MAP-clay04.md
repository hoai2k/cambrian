# Titanichthys clay04 — boundary ownership before construction

Author: Astra high. This map implements the structural proposal accepted by root after clay03's rejection. It is an authoring plan, not anatomy approval.

The anterior scaffold is no longer sampled from the longitudinal body profile. Six authored anatomical control boundaries describe the oral rim, lip backing, preoral vault/mandibular underside, cranial crown/recessed floor, occipital transition/throat, and neck. Upper, lateral and lower sectors have independent coordinates and share their exact boundary vertices. The neck boundary alone is taken from the retained posterior scaffold. The intermediate boundaries are surface cage controls, not individual raised plates.

| Patch | Boundaries it owns | Neutral form | Gape ownership |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Upper lip and preoral face | Upper oral rim → lip backing → rounded preoral arch | Short rounded rostral face above an inset edentulous margin | Skull |
| Cranial vault | Preoral arch → crown → occipital arch → neck | Broad shallow roof and gently turned plate-bearing lateral surfaces | Skull, fading at neck |
| Lateral cheeks | Oral corners → lateral vault/floor joins → neck | Substantial fixed cheek beside and behind commissure | Mainly skull; short transition at lower corner |
| Tissue-covered mandible | Lower oral rim → lip backing/underside | Narrow flattened envelope with downturned anterior connection; no separate tubular rail | Jaw hinge |
| Recessed outer floor | Mandibular underside → medial floor control → throat | Lies medial to the jaw in the anterior mouth; widens only behind hinge | Eases from jaw to fixed throat |
| Inner oral floor | Same lower oral rim → offset inner mandibular/floor surface → rear lumen | Shares the actual lip edge and compatible motion with the outer floor | Same anatomical control parameter as outer floor |
| Palate and inner cheeks | Same upper oral rim → palatal vault → rear lumen | Interior space below the cranial roof; no roof-sized solid overhang | Skull fading to fixed throat |
| Orbital socket patches | Cut bounded windows in the cheek cage → small oval apertures → recessed socket bowls | Real openings with quiet eyelid shelves and recessed globes | Same skull transform as neighbouring cheek |
| Pectorals/pelvics | Span spine + authored local chord and section profiles | Broad proximal membranes, camber, mild dihedral and twist, single tapered endpoints | No final fin rig in this phase |

The head sector patches share vertices, including the lip and neck. Orbital windows are bridged into closed recessed sockets. The mandibular region is part of the closed body/lining manifold; there is no overlapping exposed jaw tube. Cavity and outer-floor coordinates use the same anatomical parameter so corresponding edges cannot peel apart. No final skeleton or skin weights are claimed from the temporary gape study.
