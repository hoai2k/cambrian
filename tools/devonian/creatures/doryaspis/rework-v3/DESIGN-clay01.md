# Doryaspis V3 — first new clay proposal

Creative owner: Astra high. Execution owner: Terra medium. This phase is a new sculpture, not a reskin or rerun of V2. All source, output and review work stays in the two new `rework-v3` directories.

## Evidence and mouth reconciliation

The preserved user reference was inspected at its full supplied resolution. It emphasizes a deep, oval armoured front, a broad rounded roof with plate fields, generous curved cornual roots, a thin tapered saw-like projection, and a long sinuous posterior. Its attribution is unknown; the image is private appearance direction, not fossil evidence.

The actual locally preserved supplementary figure 1b of [Botella, Fariña and Huera-Huarte (2024)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-06837-8) was inspected alongside it. The figure identifies the projection as a pseudorostrum and shows the Doryaspis lateral reconstruction with a low dorsal roof and deeper ventral shield. The paper describes the fixed cephalic shield as the lifting surface and distinguishes it from the movable caudal region. Thus cornual plates remain rigid armour continuous with the shield; no pectoral hinges or ordinary paired fins are added. The dorsal spine in panel 1a belongs to Panamintaspis and is excluded.

The earlier research places the jawless oral region above the fixed ventral pseudorostrum. The user's literal request to put the mouth below that projection is not supported by that reconstruction. The supplied artistic oblique image does not resolve this anatomical reversal. We must say so candidly rather than silently create an underside mouth, false nasal opening, or hinged lower jaw.

The proposed reconciliation tests a compact anterior/upward oral recess on the front face, under the shield roof and above the projection root. Compared with the old model, it has a shorter transverse aperture and a forward component, so it should read as integrated front anatomy rather than a conspicuous hole on top. A real curved negative volume produces the chamber and its terminal wall. This is an authorial interpretation of soft anatomy and presentation; it does **not** fulfill the literal below-pseudorostrum relationship. Actual clay images must decide whether the presentation is satisfactory, and root must keep this distinction visible to the user.

## Sculpt intent

- A single continuous main body has a shallow curved roof, full peripheral shoulders, deep ventral bowl, broad posterior exit and smooth gradual taper. Large control sections provide shape from front, dorsal, side and ventral views. It is not the previous flat top with new seams applied.
- Fine shallow sculpt boundaries mark a central dorsal field and lateral/orbital transitions. These are sparse interpretations of broad plate regions, not literal turtle scutes or a honeycomb pattern. Later pigmentation may reinforce this structure only after clay approval.
- Cornual plates grow from a wide thick root, become thinner and sweep forward toward gently downturned tips. Exact solid unions make these actual continuous attachments.
- The ventral pseudorostrum is a flattened tapered blade. Small marginal triangular denticles belong to its silhouette and are not functional biting teeth. Its root is physically united with the anterior shield.
- The tail emerges with a substantial root, narrows progressively through a restrained lateral curve and enters the lower part of the closed caudal membrane. The caudal axis is hypocercal; this does not require the lower visible membrane to be the larger lobe. Graceful animation remains later work.
- Two small eye globes are embedded placeholders for clay scale and face judgment, without hoops or pads. One real branchial recess is carved on each rear flank. None of this is a final eye audit or action/socket approval.

World coordinates are **X lateral, Y up, +Z forward** throughout source, Blender and clay renderer. No export conversion is involved yet. Camera framing uses the actual projected coordinates of all subject vertices; six full views must keep a ten-percent border. The oral close view fits a declared local anatomical box and is explicitly a close-up.

## Acceptance gate

Inspect all six full images and the oral close-up before deciding on materials or rig. The whole model must be visible in every full image; front and ventral views must show substantial shield depth, the dorsal view a coherent oval shield/cornual outline, and side/oblique views a natural shoulder-to-tail transition. Look for thin glued wings, a slab front, pinched or intersecting root surfaces, an overly busy saw edge, a seam collar, or a lump where the caudal membrane meets the tail. The oral view must show one small genuine mouth with readable depth, supported roof, and fixed projection immediately beneath it. No visual pass is implied by source geometry or construction checks.

This first handoff ends with actual clay evidence. No colour textures, fine scale fields, rig, clips, sockets, full/LOD exports, audits, packaging, public replacement, Git or shared-state edits are authorized in this command group.
