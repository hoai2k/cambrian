# Review — Doryaspis V3 clay02, 12 September 2026

Four clay iterations, each built and rendered whole; renders and reports under
`<authoring>/doryaspis/rework-v3/clay02{,b,c,d,e}/`. Contact sheets in the
session scratchpad as `clay02{,b,c,d}-sheet.png`.

## What each iteration answered

**clay02** — first build of the new design. Rejected before rendering: the
oral cutter's flare had an unconstrained floor, so the boolean shaved the top
off the pseudorostrum for 0.1 units in front of the face. The section was given
independent top and bottom profiles and the flare now opens upward and
sideways only.

**clay02 (rendered)** — the mouth was correct (terminal, forward-facing, above
the saw) but the whole animal was a slender dart: shield half-width 0.62 on a
4.5-unit body, cornual plates that read as flat manta wings, no snout to speak
of, sutures too shallow to see. Measured against the reference the carapace
should be roughly 0.70 as wide as it is long, the saw about half the shield's
length and the cornual span about 1.4x it.

**clay02b** — re-proportioned to those ratios: half-width 0.80, shield 2.6
long, saw 1.4 free, cornual span 3.6. The front view now read as a snout with
a terminal mouth. Still wrong: the shield tapered evenly to a point at both
ends instead of carrying the reference's broadly rounded rear edge, the cornua
were still flat in section, and the sutures still did not read.

**clay02c** — rear edge held broad to z = -0.74 and then dropped away; cornual
chord collapsed fast once past the shield edge so the section stays oval and
they read as horns; sutures deepened to 0.029. Two close-ups were added, a
straight-on mouth view and a snout side, because the direction cannot be judged
from a three-quarter. The straight-on view was right. The profile was not: the
snout narrowed so gradually that there was no front face in silhouette.

**clay02d** — accepted. Depth held further forward so the face is a blunt 0.45
x 0.41, the saw dropped and thickened at the root so it bulges 0.07 below the
ventral line all the way from z = 1.0, and the shield's front half widened so
the muzzle is short rather than a long cone. **clay02e** is clay02d with the
suture sigma narrowed for the material stage; the volumes are identical.

## Against the direction

- *The mouth is at the very front.* It is: a transverse ellipse bored straight
  back along -Z through the middle of the front face, lip half-width 0.105,
  from y +0.074 down to y -0.038, aperture at z = 1.47 on a face that ends at
  1.53. The build asserts the cut never reaches within 0.05 of the dorsal roof,
  never runs back behind the chamber's own terminal wall, and that the rim's
  mean normal faces forward and is not tilted up or down.
- *Not on top of his head.* There is no dorsal or upward aperture anywhere. The
  V2 model's conspicuous dorsal hole is gone, and so is clay01's front/upward
  recess, which root had already held for reading as a hole on top.
- *The saw is a lower jaw protrusion, and the mouth is above it.* The saw's
  root is a broad mass buried in the ventral snout, emerging from underneath
  around z = 1.0 and still 0.208 half-width at the face against the snout's
  0.224. Its root top sits 0.063 below the mouth's lower lip.
- *The front of body is like a snout.* The shield narrows over its forward 0.5
  units into a short muzzle ending in a blunt rounded face, and the mouth sits
  in the middle of that face with rim on all four sides.

## What else the clay is judged good on

Single closed manifold component, 60k vertices, no non-manifold edges, no
degenerate faces, positive volume. Eyes seated in flank orbits at 66.5%
embedded on a 0.014 inset — seated, not buried. Cornual plates and saw at full
length. Hypocercal tail with a substantial root and a sinuous lateral axis.
Sparse shallow plate fields on a low domed shield over a deep ventral bowl.

Remaining softness, recorded rather than hidden: in pure side silhouette the
saw's lower edge continues the body's ventral line almost exactly, so the
"chin" is read from the bulge below the ventral line rather than from a step.
That is how the reference draws it, and the three-quarter and straight-on views
carry the reading, so it was accepted rather than exaggerated.
