The exposed segmented trunk, biramous limb parts and tail are represented. However:

1. The cameras render +Z vertically, so the promised horizontal swimming/legs-up
   presentation cannot be judged. Source aim uses to_track_quat('-Z','Y'), which
   does not explicitly align the camera image up with world +Y. Correct the camera
   basis with explicit world-up projection and fit actual projected geometry.
   Side crops off both head and tail; oblique/underside/cutaway touch or clip ends.
   Preserve the directly authored +Z-forward/+Y-up specimen coordinates.
2. Limb rows read as two dense, uniform comb walls rather than individually readable
   shrimp limbs, particularly side/cutaway. Preserve primary 32 pairs and 20-interval
   endopods, but assess an anatomically sensible static metachronal resting pose,
   bent/lifted leading endopods and appropriate exopod area/spacing/shape. Do not
   reduce scientifically supported count to hide the issue. The user's reference
   clearly separates jointed leg silhouettes above ovate exopods.
3. Inspect the front valve/head/limb relationships in corrected orientation before
   deciding whether shell flare, limb reach or widths need bounded changes.
4. The large globes are still obvious round attachments on narrow supports. Full
   volume eye audit remains after rework as requested, but authoring should plan
   real anatomical cup support rather than treating tiny peduncle overlap as final.
